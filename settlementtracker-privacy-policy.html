Settlement Tracker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Settlement Tracker helps you keep a personal record of money lent and borrowed. No account is required.

Your settlement data

Names, amounts, dates, remarks, and notes are stored in the app’s local database on your device. Display preferences are stored on the device. The app does not upload settlement records to a developer-operated server or send these records to the advertising SDK.

Your device’s backup service may include app data according to your Apple backup settings. Settlement Tracker does not provide its own cloud sync.

App Lock

When enabled, App Lock uses Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode through iOS. Settlement Tracker does not receive or store your biometric data or passcode. The enabled setting is saved on the device. App Lock protects the app screen after the app goes to the background; it does not encrypt the local database or exported CSV files.

CSV files you control

Export happens only when you choose it. CSV backups contain readable personal and financial information and are not encrypted by the app. The destination you select in Files may be a cloud provider. Any further storage or sharing is governed by that provider and your choices.

Import reads the file you select and adds validated records to the local database. Duplicate records are skipped and conflicting records are rejected.

Feedback and support

Settings → Send Feedback opens an email draft addressed to ndsoftwarestudio@gmail.com. The draft includes the app version, device model, iOS version, locale, and similar technical details. It does not attach settlement records. You can review and edit the draft before sending; email is sent only if you choose to send it in your email app.

Advertising and privacy choices

Settlement Tracker displays Google AdMob banner ads and uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage consent. The app disables ad personalization and publisher first-party IDs and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

Google’s SDK may process IP addresses, device or app identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic data to deliver ads and operate its services. Ads make network requests. Settlement names, notes, and amounts are not supplied as ad targeting information.

Where required, consent choices are presented before ads are requested. You can reopen available choices from Settings. Settlement tracking remains available when ads cannot load.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures for information about Google’s data practices.

Retention and deletion

Records remain until you delete them or remove the app and its data. Swipe to delete an entry or a person; deleting a person also deletes their entries. Clear list notes directly in the dashboard. Exported files and device backups must be managed separately in the places where you saved them.

Permissions and changes

The app does not request access to contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location. File access is limited to files you select through the system picker. This policy will be updated when the app’s data practices change.

Contact

For questions about this policy, email ndsoftwarestudio@gmail.com.
